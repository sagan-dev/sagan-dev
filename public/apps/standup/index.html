Why use Standup with your standing desk

Simple by default. The automation is there when you want it and quiet when you don't.

Desk control in the menu bar

Height, connection status and desk name at a glance. Hold the round buttons to move, let go to stop. No Dock icon, no extra windows.

Stand and Sit presets

One click sends the desk to your saved standing or sitting height and stops right on it. Click again to stop on the way.

Automation

Stands up before meetings

Standup reads Apple Calendar, including Outlook and Exchange accounts added to macOS, and raises the desk shortly before an online meeting you organise or have accepted. It goes back down when the meeting ends.

Optional

Waits until the call is over

Keeps the desk up while Zoom, Teams, Webex, Slack, FaceTime or your browser still uses the microphone or speaker (per app on macOS 14.2+). It only sees that audio is in use, never the audio itself.

Optional

Meeting ended early?

When the call goes quiet before the scheduled end, a notification offers to lower the desk.

Optional

Unplanned calls

When a call starts that is not in your calendar, a notification offers to raise the desk. Nothing moves until you click.

Automation

Never moves by surprise

Before the desk moves on its own it nudges twice by about 0.5 cm in the direction it is about to travel, then waits a second.

Optional

Hourly stand goal

Set how many minutes per hour you want to stand. Time you already stood, in meetings or by hand, counts, so the desk only rises for what is missing.

AppleScript

Move the desk to Stand, Sit or a set height from Shortcuts, Alfred or your own scripts.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no servers. Calendar events are read on your Mac, and the App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”.

Next version

Skip, snooze and screen lock

Skip or snooze an automatic move, and no automatic moves while your Mac is locked or asleep. Not in version 1.1.0 yet.

Frequently asked questions

Does Standup work with the IKEA IDÅSEN (Idasen) desk?

Yes. The IKEA IDÅSEN with its Bluetooth LINAK controller is the desk Standup is built and tested with.

Does it work with other LINAK desks?

Other Bluetooth desks with a LINAK controller may work, but they have not been verified. Standup is free, so you can try it with your desk.

Is Standup free?

Yes. Standup is free on the Mac App Store and needs macOS 13 or newer and a Mac with Bluetooth.

How does the desk know about my meetings?

Standup reads your calendar on the Mac through Apple's EventKit: Apple Calendar and Outlook or Exchange accounts added to macOS. It raises the desk shortly before an online meeting with other attendees that you organise or have accepted; 2 minutes before by default.

Does Standup listen to my calls?

No. Standup never records or accesses audio. If you turn on call detection, it only sees whether a meeting app is using the microphone or speaker (per app on macOS 14.2 or newer). The App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.

Will the desk move without warning?

No. Before an automatic move the desk nudges twice by about 0.5 cm and pauses for a second. For calls that are not in your calendar Standup only shows a notification; nothing moves until you click.

Can I keep using the phone app?

The desk talks to one device at a time. Close the desk app on your phone before Standup connects; afterwards Standup reconnects on its own.

Can I automate the desk?

Yes. Standup supports AppleScript, so you can move the desk to Stand or Sit or to a set height from Shortcuts, Alfred or your own scripts.
